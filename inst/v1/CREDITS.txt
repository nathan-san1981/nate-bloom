Nate Bloom studio instruments (inst/v1)

Each instrument is one MP3 file (<id>.mp3): single notes, trimmed, levelled and encoded at 128 kbit/s, back to
back. manifest.json lists every note: MIDI note, name, key range, loop start/end (seconds) for the sustained
instruments, and where the note sits in the instrument's file. The app crossfades each loop after decoding.
