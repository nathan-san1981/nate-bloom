Nate Bloom studio instruments (inst/v1)

Each instrument here is a set of single notes, trimmed, levelled and encoded to 128 kbit/s MP3 for download
on demand. manifest.json lists every note: MIDI note, file, key range, and loop start/end (seconds) for the
sustained instruments. The app crossfades each loop after decoding.
