OpenTTD 15.3 for tablets, two editions built from the same code:
- UnicornRailways-*.apk: Unicorn Railways for the Fire 7 Kids (kid layer coming next; for now the stock game with touch).
- OpenTTD-tablet-*.apk: the full game for the Galaxy Tab S10 FE+, interface sized from the screen's density.

Touch: tap = click, drag one finger = drag the map or draw track/road, press and hold = right click (tooltips),
pinch = zoom, two fingers = scroll, two-finger tap or Back = cancel the tool or close the front window.
