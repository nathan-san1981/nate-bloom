Unicorn Railways for her Fire (UnicornRailways-*.apk): OpenTTD with her own big buttons instead of the menus.
Rounded pink, lilac, blue, yellow and green buttons, each with a picture and a word: Road and Track (drag a finger
to draw), Bus stop and Station (tap a road or a rail), Bus! and Train! (one tap: builds the depot, buys the vehicle,
sends it between two of her stops), Oops (removes only things she built), Fast, Closer and Farther, and Go home
(saves her world and goes back to the clock). Her buses and trains are pink.
Nothing she can lose: unlimited money, nothing breaks down, no disasters, no rival companies, no news pop-ups.
Her world saves when she leaves, every 10 minutes, and when the tablet sleeps. Reopening after Go home starts clean. A new world opens on the town; nothing red pops up when something can't be built.

Also here: OpenTTD-tablet-*.apk, the full game with touch controls, with bigger buttons and text for a big tablet.
