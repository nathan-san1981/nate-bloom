Nate Bloom for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

**Install:** download **NateBloom-Setup.exe** and run it. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It installs for you (no administrator password), adds a Start menu entry and opens.

**No install:** **NateBloom-portable.exe** runs from wherever you put it, a USB stick included.

What's in it: every synth engine, the Studio instruments, Nate Bloom's own plugins (and Nathan's, marked NATHAN'S), the sampler, Beat Pads, the sequencer, Rhythm Creator, DJ, Garage and Tracks, MIDI keyboards and controllers over USB, Favorites, works offline, saves to Downloads\Nate Bloom, and an "App ↓" button when a newer version is out.

Not in the Windows app yet (the Linux app has them): loading your own VST3/CLAP plugins and the free-plugin store, the Bitwig bridge, the multithreaded native engine (so no Alpha Juno engine), the low-latency pads player.
