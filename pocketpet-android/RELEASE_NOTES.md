Pocket Pet for the Galaxy Tab S10 FE+: an original virtual pet with the classic rules. Hatch an egg and raise it
through baby, child, teen and adult with meals, snacks, the left-or-right game, cleaning, medicine, lights out and
discipline. Care decides how it grows up, and a neglected or very old pet dies. Ten characters (unicorn, dragon,
kitty, bunny, puppy, penguin, octopus, alien, robot, dino) and six shell colors. Time passes while it's closed, and
a notification tells you when your pet calls. A, B, C buttons work like the original, or tap the icons directly.
