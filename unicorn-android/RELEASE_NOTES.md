Unicorn Clock for Fire tablets (and any Android tablet). Free and open source (MIT).

The unicorn weather clock with everything built in: works offline, opens by itself when the tablet turns on,
stays pinned on the clock (exit is behind the grown-up PIN), dims the real backlight at bedtime, and reads
typed reminders aloud with the tablet's own voice.

Install: on the Fire, allow apps from unknown sources, open UnicornClock.apk, then open Unicorn Clock.
