Unicorn Clock for Fire tablets (and any Android tablet). Free and open source (MIT).

The unicorn weather clock with everything built in: works offline, opens by itself when the tablet turns on,
comes straight back if Home or Recents is pressed (grown-up settings are the way out), dims the real
backlight at bedtime, and reads typed reminders aloud with the tablet's own voice.

Install on a Fire: Settings → Security & Privacy → Apps from Unknown Sources → on for Silk Browser.
Download UnicornClock.apk below in Silk, open it, Install, then open Unicorn Clock.
Full setup guide: https://github.com/nathan-san1981/nate-bloom/tree/main/unicorn-android

First time: set a Grown-up PIN in grown-up settings, then Tablet app → Let it open by itself.

New in this version: Unicorn Meadow (a cozy game where unicorn friends build their meadow), and a kid-sized
Unicorn Dungeon: a big Pack button with pictures of each item, arrows you can hold to keep walking, a life bar,
Yes / No questions you can hear, tap-the-words-to-hear messages in the clock's natural voice, and calm music.

Checked on Fire-sized Android 9 and Android 11 emulators (what Fire OS 7 and 8 are built on): clock, backlight
dimming, voice, volume, comes back after Home/Recents and after being swiped away, Back key, pinning, offline,
games, Unicorn Music, opening at start-up, crash recovery (and giving up after 3 crashes in 10 minutes), updates
keeping her data, and optional device-owner lockdown with undo.
