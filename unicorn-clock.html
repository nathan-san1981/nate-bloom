Grown-up settings

Grown-up PIN

The master PIN is your spare key: it always unlocks the clock, and it is needed to change or turn off the grown-up PIN. Write it down somewhere safe. The grown-up PIN is the one you type day to day (it can be the same as the master).

Sleep & wake

Outfit temperatures (°F, today's high)

Anything above "Warm" is Hot. After 4 PM the outfit card switches to tomorrow, for laying clothes out at night.

Location

Bedtime screen

What she can open at bedtime (buttons on the bedtime screen):
The "Grown-ups" button on the bedtime screen (PIN) can pause bedtime for 15 minutes, an hour, or until wake-up.

Screen & play

In Fully Kiosk (with its JavaScript interface on) these set the real screen brightness. Anywhere else the screen is shaded instead. Open these settings by holding the clock for 3 seconds.

Sound check

Dusk & dawn dimming

Sleep sounds

The sounds are made on the tablet, so they work without internet. A lullaby plays first, then the sleep sound takes over. "All night" fades out at wake time, and timed sounds fade out over 30 seconds. In Fully Kiosk, turn on Autoplay audio so sounds can start at bedtime on their own.

Alarms

Tap the day circles to pick days. Voice notes recorded in Notes show up as alarm sounds. Alarms ring on any screen, even at bedtime.

Calendar & countdowns

Birthdays, appointments and special days are added on the Calendar screen (tap a day, then "Add to this day"). Adding needs the PIN if one is set. The nearest one counts down on the clock.

Star chart

Jobs, which days they count, the weekly goal and the reward are edited on the Star chart screen with the "Edit jobs" button (PIN protected).

Backup

A backup holds settings, notes, drawings, voice notes, stars, alarms and calendar dates. Save one now and then, and after a restore the clock reloads.
