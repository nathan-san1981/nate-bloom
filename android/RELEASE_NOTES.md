Nate Bloom as an Android app, made for the Galaxy Tab S10 FE+.

**Install:** download **NateBloom.apk** below on the tablet, open it, and allow installs from your browser or Files app when Android asks. Later versions install over this one and keep your sounds, songs and settings.

What the app adds to the website:
- **Fast pads.** Beat Pads taps play from Android's low-latency audio (AAudio) instead of the browser's, so the pads answer much sooner. The ⚡ button next to FX shows the delay, says why fast pads are paused when they are (FX or EQ on Beat Pads, 16 Levels, Repeat, a solo, a Tracks or stems recording), and turns them off. Takes and Keep that still record every hit where you played it.
- **MIDI over USB through Android itself.** Plug in the K.O. II, then open MIDI → Connect. Its pads trigger fast pads too.
- **⚡ Fast plugins** (top of Plugins): your own plugins (YOURS) play from Android's low-latency audio like the fast pads, the same DSP compiled into the app, so they answer much sooner when you play them live. While it's on, Nate Bloom's effects, Takes and Tracks don't hear them.
- **Link with the Linux app** on the same Wi-Fi: ☰ → **Link** → **Find computers**, pick the computer and type the code it shows (only the first time; after that it links by itself). Send projects either way, and **Play together**: one tempo, and Play and Stop start and stop both at the same moment.
- **Keeps playing in the background** when you switch apps or the screen turns off, with a notification while it plays.
- **Storage the browser can't clear:** your projects, samples and settings live inside the app.
- **Saving goes to Downloads/Nate Bloom** (backups, WAVs, stems, MIDI files).
- **Full screen**, with the screen kept on while the app is open. Android Back closes the open panel.

The app runs the same Nate Bloom as the website and updates with it. It needs the internet the first time it starts, then works offline.
